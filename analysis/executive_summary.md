**Headline:** Pune's low revenue is a footfall gap, not a stockout gap. Do not fund the Pune stock-availability programme yet.

**Magnitude:** Pune's revenue is 26.14% below the all-store mean daily revenue and its footfall is 25.00% below the all-store mean footfall. Revenue per footfall is only 1.52% below the all-store figure (Pune 419.29 against 425.75).

**What is driving it and what is not:** Footfall tracks revenue closely (r = 0.735 in Pune). Stockouts do not account for Pune's gap: it ranks 4th of 5 (2.32 hours; Hyderabad highest at 3.85), and revenue vs stockout hours is only r = -0.254 in Pune. Correlation is not causation.
